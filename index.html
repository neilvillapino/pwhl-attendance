Is the PWHL's growth real? Attendance is up, but expansion tells part of the story

By Neil Villapiano | 12/1, 2026

The biggest thing that stood out to met when looking at this data was the consistent growth of the league's attendance each season. The growth of women's hockey has been tremendous and the PWHL has been one of the biggest beneficiaries of that. Attendance grew every season, from 5,448 per game in 2023-24 to 9,304 per game in 2025-26.

The reason this matters is because the growth of not just women's hockey, but women's sports has grown so much over the past few years. The investment from not just the people owning the league and teams, but the fans as well. This also shows in the expansion of the league which added Seattle and Vancouver, and the league played more games.

There's a Like-for-like growth when it comes to the league and its fans. Without the Takeover Tour and the two expansion teams, the six original teams averaged 7,736 fans, up about 18%. It's important that expansion eventually happened. It creates more fans, bigger attendance, a wider audience overall, and more money.

Another point that's impressive is the growth of attendance between the original teams in the league and the expansion teams. For example, Seattle, playing at Climate Pledge Arena, led at 12,875 per game. The New York Sirens, playing at the Prudential Center averaged 5,095.

Without question, the biggest factor to this boom is what happened with the 2026 Milano/Cortina Winter Olympics. There's differences before and after the hockey tournament concluded. The U.S. markets averaged 6,684 before the Olympics and 9,368 after, about 40% higher. The question now is whether or not this hype will last and create consistent quote after the Olympics.

Jane McManus, a New York University professor at the Tisch Institute for Global Sport, feels strongly about the PWHL's fast expanision when it comes to the growth of the league and sport.. McManus told The Associated Press "“Would you tell a men’s league to go slow if they saw a real upside in a developing market? You just wouldn’t.”

So what is the next step for the PWHL? It's more expansion. The league is adding teams in Detroit, San Jose, Hamilton (Ontario, Canada) and Las Vegas. This move proves again that this league and womne's hockey is here and is not going anywhere anytime soon.

Sources and methodology

Attendance figures are average fans per regular-season game. League-wide season averages and totals come from the PWHL's season-end release. Team averages come from The Hockey News and exclude neutral-site Takeover Tour games. Percent changes were recalculated from the published numbers.
